## Open before submission

1. **Apple developer team and profiles.** App IDs `me.somalabs.flight` and `me.somalabs.flight.widgets` with Sign in with Apple, Push Notifications and the App Group `group.me.somalabs.flight`; provisioning profiles for both targets on iOS and for the Mac app (Sign in with Apple and push; the Mac Debug build signs to run locally without them). Not touched by Task 12.
2. **Blocker: privacy policy and support pages return 404.** Settings links to `https://somalabs.me/legal/soma-flight-privacy` (page written 2026-11-01, live once the landing PR merges) and `https://somalabs.me/support`; on 2026-10-29 both answered **404** (after a redirect to www). Publish both, then enter the privacy policy URL and the support URL in App Store Connect (both are required fields). The policy must cover: email and user id, followed flights and flight history, push tokens, rate-limit records, FlightAware as the data source, deletion and export.
3. **Sign in with Apple token revocation on account deletion: built, waiting on the key.** Settings, Delete Account asks an Apple account to sign in with Apple once more and sends that authorization code to `delete-account`, which exchanges it at `https://appleid.apple.com/auth/token` and revokes the refresh token at `/auth/revoke` before anything is erased (`_shared/apple.ts`). Nothing from Apple is stored. Owner action: create a Sign in with Apple key in the developer account, then set the secrets `APPLE_TEAM_ID`, `APPLE_SIGNIN_KEY_ID` and `APPLE_SIGNIN_PRIVATE_KEY` (the .p8 text; `APPLE_CLIENT_ID` defaults to `me.somalabs.flight`) and deploy `delete-account`. Until the secrets exist the deployed function refuses Apple accounts with 503 rather than skip the revoke; email accounts are unaffected.
4. **App Store Connect privacy answers ("data use disclosure").** Match the manifests: Contact Info, Email Address; Identifiers, User ID; Identifiers, Device ID (iOS push tokens); User Content, Other User Content; Usage Data, Product Interaction (rate-limit records). All linked to the user, none used for tracking, purpose App Functionality. No analytics, no diagnostics SDK.
5. **AeroAPI commercial terms.** The app must ship on the Standard tier (B2C distribution), not Personal; the full terms (attribution, caching and retention, redistribution) were not read. See `docs/provider-check.md`.
6. **Release configuration.** Release fails closed without `Config.local.xcconfig` (Supabase URL and anon key); create it on the archiving machine, in both `Apps/iOS/` and `Apps/macOS/`. On 2026-10-31 neither file existed in the Mac checkout, so an archive made there would open on "no server configured" and be rejected in review.
6b. **App name.** The App Store name is unique across the whole store. If "Soma Flight" is taken when creating the App Store Connect record, use a subtitle-style name such as "Soma Flight: Flight Tracker"; the name under the icon (`CFBundleDisplayName`) can stay "Soma Flight".
7. **Age rating.** Fill the questionnaire: no objectionable content, no user-to-user communication, no web browsing; expected 4+.
8. **Review notes.** A demo account (email sign-in) with a few followed flights, since every screen needs an account on the backend.
9. **Real-device checks still open from earlier tasks.** Real APNs pushes and Live Activity updates, a cold-start notification tap, Keychain under the Mac sandbox, Sign in with Apple on a device, and real taps on the export and deletion rows (the share sheet and the dialog were not driven in this pass).

## Owner actions for v1.1 (2026-10-29, Task 24)

These block turning production on, not building. Until they are done every v1.1 screen runs on fakes and recorded fixtures, production shows monograms and our own side view renderer, and nothing is bought or signed.

1. **Apple developer agreement.** The account holder accepts the current Program License Agreement in the developer account (and the Paid Apps agreement in App Store Connect if the app ever charges). Until then signed builds, provisioning profiles and uploads are refused.
2. **Logo licence.** Request quotes and sign the logo licence (Airhex, or another provider behind `LogoProvider`); then set `LOGO_PROVIDER` and `AIRHEX_KEY` as Supabase project secrets. The Wikimedia Commons library (`assets/logos/commons`) stands in until then.
3. **Commons logos and trademarks.** The Commons files are public domain or CC licensed as artwork (credited in Acknowledgements; the KLM crown is CC BY-SA 3.0 and our crop of it, a tighter viewBox, carries the same licence), but the marks stay the airlines' trademarks. A copyright licence does not grant trademark use: counsel confirms showing them, or the licensed provider replaces them.
4. **Side view provider.** Choose the source (Logostream API or an Airliners Illustrated direct licence), request quotes and sign; then the concrete `SideViewProvider` adapter is written against its delivery format and `SIDE_VIEW_PROVIDER` is set.
5. **Airline names, logos, colours and liveries.** The providers or counsel confirm that showing them in a commercial app is covered (spec 4.3 legal note). Our renderer never draws a trademark itself (no tail symbol without a licensed or Commons asset).
6. **AeroAPI cost and deploy approval.** Approve the AeroAPI cost of the arrival forecast and the inbound plane chain: both are wired to real data through `flight-insights` (cached, under the daily spend cap), on in Debug and, since the owner's yes on 2026-11-06, in Release too (`INSIGHTS_ENABLED = YES`); until the AeroAPI key is set they answer unavailable and the sections say so, and the history endpoint's price is still to confirm (see `docs/api-contract.md`, flight-insights). Approve the deploy itself: backend plan Task 10 (create or link the Supabase project, set the APNs and AeroAPI keys as secrets, `supabase db push`, `supabase functions deploy`, register the AeroAPI webhook). Nothing paid is created without that yes.
7. **Acknowledgements.** Present in the app (Settings, Acknowledgements): circle-flags (MIT), Natural Earth (public domain), Wikidata (CC0), OurAirports (public domain) and every Commons logo with its author and licence. Check the list again when the licensed providers replace Commons.
8. **Privacy and support pages.** The privacy policy lives at `https://somalabs.me/legal/soma-flight-privacy`; `https://somalabs.me/support` still answers 404 (item 2 above); both URLs are required in App Store Connect. The policy also covers the v1.1 fields (booking code, seat, notes, reason for travel).
9. **Apple token revocation on account deletion.** Still open (item 3 above): `delete-account` must call Apple's revoke endpoint with the Sign in with Apple client secret.
10. **Asiana alliance.** The seed keeps Asiana (OZ) in Star Alliance until 2026-12-16; after its merger into Korean Air it moves to SkyTeam (or its own row ends). Update `supabase/seed/airlines.csv`, regenerate the seed and the app JSON, then ship.
